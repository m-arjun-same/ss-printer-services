01

The problem

A cheap top-up costs more than a refill

A cartridge refilled without cleaning carries old toner, worn blades and tired seals into the next cycle — streaks, leaks and grey backgrounds follow. The fix is not more toner; it is the full process, in the right order.

The refill process

Five steps, in order

Step 01

Disassemble

The cartridge is opened up, not just uncorked.

Step 02

Clean

Residual toner and ink are cleaned out fully.

Step 03

Replace worn parts

Worn components are swapped before fresh toner goes in.

Step 04

Refill

Filled with the correct ink or toner for the cartridge.

Step 05

Print test

A test page proves density and cleanliness before handover.

02

The outcome

Tested before it leaves the counter

No cartridge leaves on a promise. The print test is part of the service — if the test page is not clean, the cartridge goes back on the bench, not into your bag. Popular cartridges can sell out at busy times, reviewers note — a quick call ahead reserves yours.
