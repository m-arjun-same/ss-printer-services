A salon chair that Pune has trusted since 2012.

Mariglow Salon began in 2012, when founder Meera Kulkarni started styling hair out of a two-chair studio in Koregaon Park. Fourteen years on, the salon on Lane 7 still runs the same way: consult honestly, treat gently, mirror-check before you leave.

Hair repair, colour refresh, skin and spa rituals, at-home styling and glow memberships — for regulars, brides and busy weekends across 12 neighbourhoods of Pune.

The practice, since 2012

  • Hair, skin and nail care with Botanic Locks, Velvet Skin, Herb & Hue, Pure Petal, Silk Root, Glow Theory, Amber Oil and Rose Clay.
  • The salon’s own guest book counts 40,000+ appointments since 2012 — our claim, kept exactly as we state it.
  • At-home and bridal styling with teams that travel across Pune.
  • Every colour refresh follows the full studio process: consult, cleanse, patch-test, apply, then a mirror check before you leave the chair.
  • Bridal mornings, party styling and glow memberships for guests who celebrate every season — from Koregaon Park and Kalyani Nagar to Baner and Kothrud.
  • Genuine care lines — hair oils, colour care, skin rituals and nail care — twelve enquiry-only lines, priced at the shelf, never invented online.

The problem

A rushed colour costs more than a refresh

Hair coloured without cleansing carries old product, tired ends and uneven tones into the next cycle — bands, brassiness and dull lengths follow. The fix is not more colour; it is the full process, in the right order.

The refresh process

Five steps, in order

Step 01

Consult

The colour story is mapped, not just mixed.

Step 02

Cleanse

Build-up and old tones are cleansed out fully.

Step 03

Patch-test

A strand test and skin patch come before fresh colour goes on.

Step 04

Apply

Colour mixed for your hair history and the look you chose.

Step 05

Gloss check

A mirror check proves shine and tone before you leave.

02

The outcome
